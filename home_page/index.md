## What this is

A Lean 4 formalisation of the limits of camera-constrained quadrotor pursuit:
when can a quadrotor with a single (optionally hinged) camera keep an airborne
target in view in every frame, given bounded thrust, drag, tilt, yaw and hinge
rates, a finite field of view and a pixel budget? The goal is a single master
inequality relating these parameters, sufficient as stated and necessary up to
an explicit gap.
