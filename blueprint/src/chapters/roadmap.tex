\chapter{Open directions}
\label{chap:roadmap}

\begin{remark}[Smoothing family and paper numbers]
A filter family $h^{(N)}$ with four frames of lag, $H_3=O(N^3)$ and $\mathrm{TV}=O(1/N)$
makes the torque-versus-lag trade-off explicit: the position error grows like
$j(N\delta)^3$ while the torque-relevant term shrinks like $1/N$. Evaluating the
budget for reference quadrotors and choosing $N$ gives the paper's numbers.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Weaving drones]
The steady-chase window constrains frames in which the drone holds its velocity. A
drone faster than the target could weave. Averaging the translational dynamics shows
that its mean forward thrust is at least about $c_Dv_T^2$, so the tilt cannot be
avoided on average. Turning this into a per-frame visibility bound is open.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Heading-locked formations]
For manoeuvring targets, only the overhead formation is proved sufficient. Holding
an offset that rotates with the target's heading (chasing from behind) costs extra
acceleration of order $d\omega_T^2$, and is the natural next sufficiency theorem.
\end{remark}
